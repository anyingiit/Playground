Good catch from the bot review: the fit check ran before `window.completion.col_offset` was applied, so with a negative offset the menu could be pushed further left than needed. The new commit applies the offset first and checks the fit at the column the menu actually opens at. In the example (80 columns, cursor at 20, 72-wide menu, `col_offset = -10`) it now opens at column 7 and ends at the last column instead of opening at 0. I added a spec for that case.
