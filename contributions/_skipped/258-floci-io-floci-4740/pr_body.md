## Summary

`CreateEventSourceMapping` used `toInt(request.get("BatchSize"), 10)`, so every mapping created without `BatchSize` got 10. AWS uses 10 only for SQS; Kinesis, DynamoDB Streams, Kafka, Amazon MQ and DocumentDB default to 100. A Kinesis or DynamoDB Streams mapping therefore reported (and polled with) `BatchSize: 10` where AWS reports 100.

This adds a small `defaultBatchSize(eventSourceArn)` helper in `LambdaService`: 10 when the source ARN is an SQS queue, 100 otherwise (self-managed Kafka has no ARN and gets 100). An explicit `BatchSize` still wins, and `UpdateEventSourceMapping` is unchanged. The ESM section of `docs/services/lambda.md` now states the defaults.
