## Description

`ceiling/1` converted its result through `Double` and `toLong()`. Results beyond the 64-bit range therefore saturated (`X is ceiling(1.0e30)` gave `9223372036854775807`), and large reals also lost precision in the `Double` round-trip.

As suggested in the issue, `Ceiling` now mirrors `Floor`: the real's `BigDecimal` value is rounded with `setScale(0, RoundingMode.CEILING).toBigInteger()`. Integer inputs are still returned unchanged.

A new `testIsCeilingOfLargeReal` in the shared `TestIs` conformance suite (wired into `TestPrologIs` and `TestProblogIs`) checks:
- `X is ceiling(1.0e30)` gives `1000000000000000000000000000000`;
- `X is ceiling(-12345678901234567890.5)` gives `-12345678901234567890`, i.e. it still rounds towards positive infinity beyond the `Long` range.

On `master` the first assertion fails with `9223372036854775807`, exactly as reported; with the fix it passes.
